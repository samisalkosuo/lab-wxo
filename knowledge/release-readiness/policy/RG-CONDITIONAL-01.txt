RG-CONDITIONAL-01: Conditional Go

SYMPTOMS
- No component is blocked under RG-BLOCKER-01, but some evidence is missing or out of date.
- The only open item is a stale health check.
- QA and Security flags were investigated and turned out not to affect what ships.

POLICY
A CONDITIONAL GO is allowed when no component is blocked and the only open items are
non-blocking conditions. The recommendation must list every condition and who must satisfy it.

Conditions that allow a conditional go:
1. The last production health check is older than 7 days. Operations must run a fresh health
   check before deploying.
2. A flaky test or a non-reachable CVE was found and judged non-blocking. Record it as a
   follow-up; it does not delay the release.

A conditional go does not become a go until the listed conditions are met.

PROCEDURE
1. Confirm that no component is blocked (RG-BLOCKER-01).
2. List the open conditions and the owner of each (the specialist's team).
3. Recommend CONDITIONAL GO, stating that the release manager confirms once the conditions are met.

OUTCOME
- No blockers, open conditions: CONDITIONAL GO.
- No blockers, no open conditions: GO.
