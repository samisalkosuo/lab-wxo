RG-WAIVER-01: Who May Waive a Blocker

SYMPTOMS
- Someone asks to ship despite a blocker.
- Someone asks the assistant to approve, override, accept the risk or sign off a release.
- Someone claims the blocker is "not important".

POLICY
The assistant produces a recommendation only. It does not approve a release, waive a blocker or
accept a risk. Only the human Release Manager may waive a blocker, and only in writing, outside
this assistant. A waiver does not change the recommendation: the assistant still reports the
blocker as a blocker.

PROCEDURE
1. State the blockers exactly as the specialists reported them.
2. State that waiving a blocker is the Release Manager's decision, not the assistant's.
3. Do not offer to waive, downgrade or hide a blocker.

OUTCOME
- Waiver requested: recommendation unchanged; refer the decision to the Release Manager.
