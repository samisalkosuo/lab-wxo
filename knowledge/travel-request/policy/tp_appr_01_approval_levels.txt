DOCUMENT: TP-APPR-01
TITLE: Approval levels by total cost of the trip
NOTE: This is a fictional agency policy written for a demo. It is not a real policy.

When this policy applies
The trip has been found to be a business trip and its estimated total cost is known. The question is who approves the trip.

Total cost
The total cost is the estimated reimbursement (per diem, travel costs and lodging) from the tool travel_estimate_reimbursement. Do not calculate the amount yourself.

Approval levels
1. No separate approval (level none): a domestic trip with no leave days, a total cost of at most 600 euros, and enough travel budget in the cost center.
2. Manager approval (level manager): the trip is abroad, or leave days are attached, or the total cost is over 600 euros but at most 2500 euros.
3. Director approval (level director): the total cost is over 2500 euros, or the remaining travel budget of the cost center is not enough (policy TP-BUDGET-01).

If more than one condition is met, the highest level applies.

Procedure
Tell the traveler the level that is needed and the reason. Name the approver: the manager comes from the employee data (travel_get_employee_profile), the director from the cost center data (travel_get_budget_status). The request id is given only after the approval has been recorded in the approval flow (travel_submit_request).
