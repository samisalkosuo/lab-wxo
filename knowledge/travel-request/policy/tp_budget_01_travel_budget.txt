DOCUMENT: TP-BUDGET-01
TITLE: Travel budget and exceeding the budget
NOTE: This is a fictional agency policy written for a demo. It is not a real policy.

When this policy applies
The estimated cost of a trip is higher than the remaining travel budget of the employee's cost center, or the question is whether the budget is enough.

Rules
Every cost center has a yearly travel budget. Check the budget with the tool travel_get_budget_status and give its result as it is. Do not compare the numbers yourself.

If the budget is not enough
The trip is not automatically denied. It needs the approval of the director of the cost center (level director, policy TP-APPR-01). The traveler must explain why the trip is necessary this year.

If the budget is enough
The budget alone does not decide the approval level. The approval level is set by policy TP-APPR-01.
