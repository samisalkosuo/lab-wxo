DOCUMENT ID: OVE-POL-01
TITLE: Statutory OVE Minimum Age Thresholds by Birth Cohort
APPLIES TO: Finnish Earnings-Related Pension Schemes (TyEL, JuEL, YEL, MYEL)

1. SYMPTOMS & INQUIRY CONTEXT
- A member inquires about the earliest date or age they can begin withdrawing a Partial Early Old-Age Pension (Osittainen varhennettu vanhuuseläke - OVE).
- A member born in 1964 or later asks why they cannot start OVE at age 61.
- A member under their statutory cohort age threshold attempts to file an OVE application.

2. STATUTORY ELIGIBILITY AGE BRACKETS BY BIRTH YEAR
Under Finnish pension legislation (TyEL 15 §), the earliest age for OVE is linked to the member's birth year:
- Born in 1958-1963: Earliest OVE eligibility age is 61 years 0 months.
- Born in 1964: Earliest OVE eligibility age is 62 years 0 months. Full retirement age is 64 years 0 months.
- Born in 1965: Earliest OVE eligibility age is 62 years 0 months. Full retirement age is 64 years 6 months.
- Born in 1966: Earliest OVE eligibility age is 62 years 0 months. Full retirement age is 64 years 9 months.
- Born in 1967: Earliest OVE eligibility age is 62 years 0 months. Full retirement age is 65 years 0 months.
- Born in 1968 and later: Earliest OVE age is set at 3 years prior to the cohort's statutory full retirement age.

3. ADVISORY PROCEDURE & ACTION
- Verify the member's date of birth and birth year using the member profile tool.
- Compare the member's current age against the statutory minimum OVE age for their cohort.
- If the member is UNDER the minimum age:
  * Inform the member clearly of their statutory minimum age and shortfall months.
  * Cite OVE-POL-01 as the governing policy rule.
  * Explain the earliest calendar date when an application can be accepted.
  * Do NOT proceed with an application flow.
