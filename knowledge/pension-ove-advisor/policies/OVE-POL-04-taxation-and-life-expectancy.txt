DOCUMENT ID: OVE-POL-04
TITLE: Life Expectancy Coefficient Adjustment and Taxation Principles
APPLIES TO: Finnish Earnings-Related Pension Schemes (TyEL, JuEL, YEL, MYEL)

1. SYMPTOMS & INQUIRY CONTEXT
- A member asks why their accrued pension amount is slightly adjusted downwards in the final forecast.
- A member inquires how pension income is taxed compared to earned wage income when received simultaneously.
- A member asks what tax card is needed when receiving both salary and OVE.

2. LIFE EXPECTANCY COEFFICIENT (ELINAIKAKERROIN)
- Statutory purpose: Adjusts the monthly pension amount to reflect changes in average life expectancy for each birth cohort, ensuring financial sustainability of the earnings-related pension system.
- Application: Fixed at age 62 for each birth cohort by the Ministry of Social Affairs and Health (STM).
- Calculation: Multiplies the drawn OVE baseline and untouched pension capital.

3. TAXATION OF COMBINED SALARY AND PENSION
- Progression impact: In Finland, both salary and pension income are subject to progressive state taxation and municipal taxation.
- Separate tax withholding rates: The Tax Administration (Verohallinto) issues separate withholding percentages for salary income and pension income.
- Advisory note: Remind the member to update their tax card with the Tax Administration upon receiving an OVE decision to avoid under-withholding (jäännösvero).

4. ADVISORY PROCEDURE & ACTION
- Show the life expectancy coefficient applied to the calculation.
- Cite OVE-POL-04 when discussing statutory coefficient adjustments and tax card guidance.
