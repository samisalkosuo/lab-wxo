DOCUMENT ID: OVE-POL-02
TITLE: OVE Draw Percentages, Irreversibility, and Benefit Exclusions
APPLIES TO: Finnish Earnings-Related Pension Schemes (TyEL, JuEL, YEL, MYEL)

1. SYMPTOMS & INQUIRY CONTEXT
- A member asks what percentage of their accrued pension they can withdraw.
- A member wants to withdraw 10%, 30%, 75%, or 100% partial pension.
- A member currently receiving another statutory pension benefit asks if they can combine it with OVE.
- A member asks if an OVE decision can be cancelled or modified retrospectively.

2. STATUTORY DRAW RULES
- Allowable withdrawal percentages: Exactly 25% or 50% of the pension accrued by the end of the previous calendar year.
- Step-up option: A member who begins with a 25% draw may later increase their draw to 50%. However, a member drawing 50% CANNOT reduce their draw back to 25%.
- Irreversibility: Once granted and paid, an OVE election cannot be cancelled or retroactively revoked.

3. STATUTORY EXCLUSIONS & BENEFIT CONFLICTS
Under TyEL 16 §, OVE CANNOT be granted if the applicant is concurrently receiving:
- Full or partial disability pension (Työkyvyttömyyseläke)
- Cash rehabilitation benefit / temporary disability pension (Kuntoutustuki)
- Years-of-service pension (Työuraeläke)
- Old-age pension (Vanhuuseläke)

4. ADVISORY PROCEDURE & ACTION
- Validate the requested draw percentage (must be 25% or 50%).
- Check member profile for any active conflicting pension benefits.
- If a conflicting benefit exists (e.g. active Kuntoutustuki):
  * State the statutory conflict clearly.
  * Cite OVE-POL-02.
  * Refuse the OVE application.
