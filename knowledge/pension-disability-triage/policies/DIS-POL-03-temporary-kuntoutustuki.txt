DIS-POL-03 — Temporary Cash Rehabilitation Benefit (Kuntoutustuki)
Source: Finnish Earnings-Related Pension Acts (TyEL § 35) — statutory summary for internal case handler use.

1. OVERVIEW
-----------
Kuntoutustuki (temporary disability pension / rehabilitation cash benefit) is a
time-limited benefit granted when:
  a. The claimant meets the incapacity threshold (work capacity <= 40%), but
  b. The incapacity is expected to be temporary (prognosis period: 1 to 12 months), AND
  c. A concrete treatment or rehabilitation plan exists that addresses the condition.

Kuntoutustuki is NOT a permanent pension. It is a bridge benefit during structured
treatment or rehabilitation, with the expectation that the claimant will return to
work or undergo reassessment at the end of the benefit period.

2. ELIGIBILITY CONDITIONS
--------------------------
All of the following must be met simultaneously:

  a. Work capacity <= 40% (same medical threshold as full disability pension, DIS-POL-01).
  b. The treating physician's B-statement specifies a realistic recovery prognosis
     within a defined timeframe (typically 6–18 months).
  c. A concrete treatment plan (hoitosuunnitelma) or rehabilitation plan
     (kuntoutussuunnitelma) has been prepared and attached to the claim.
  d. The qualifying months condition (36/60) must be met (see DIS-POL-04).
  e. Rehabilitation before pension principle assessed: if vocational rehabilitation
     is viable, it must be initiated concurrently or instead (see DIS-POL-02).

3. BENEFIT DURATION
--------------------
  - Minimum period: 1 calendar month.
  - Maximum period: As specified in the treatment/rehabilitation plan, up to 3 years
    in exceptional cases with multiple treatment phases.
  - The benefit is reviewed at the end of each granted period. If the claimant has
    not recovered, a new assessment determines whether:
      i.  The benefit is extended (new plan required).
      ii. The claimant is referred to vocational rehabilitation (DIS-POL-02).
      iii.The case is converted to a full disability pension (DIS-POL-01).

4. TREATMENT PLAN REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------
The treatment plan must be prepared by the treating specialist (not a GP) and must:
  - State the diagnosis and ICD-10 code.
  - Specify the planned treatments, procedures, or rehabilitation interventions.
  - Estimate the expected duration of the benefit period.
  - Include a realistic recovery goal (e.g. return to modified work, partial recovery).
  - Be signed and dated by the specialist.

A treatment plan older than 3 months requires updating.

5. INTERACTION WITH VOCATIONAL REHABILITATION
----------------------------------------------
Kuntoutustuki and vocational rehabilitation (DIS-POL-02) can run concurrently:
  - A claimant may receive kuntoutustuki while undergoing a workplace trial
    (tyokokeilu) or a retraining programme.
  - If the rehabilitation succeeds, kuntoutustuki ends.
  - If it fails, the case is reassessed for a permanent disability pension.

6. WHAT KUNTOUTUSTUKI IS NOT
------------------------------
  - It is NOT a permanent pension and does not convert automatically to one.
  - It does NOT accrue pension rights in the same way as active employment.
  - It does NOT replace the need for a qualifying months check.
  - It does NOT apply to cases where incapacity is expected to be permanent from
    the outset (those go directly to the full disability pension pathway, DIS-POL-01).

---------------------
