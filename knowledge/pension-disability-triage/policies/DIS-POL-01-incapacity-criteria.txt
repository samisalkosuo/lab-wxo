DIS-POL-01 — Incapacity Criteria for Disability Pension (Työkyvyttömyyseläke)
Source: Finnish Earnings-Related Pension Acts (TyEL, YEL, JuEL) — statutory summary for internal case handler use.

1. OVERVIEW
-----------
A disability pension (työkyvyttömyyseläke) may be granted to an insured person
whose ability to earn a living has been reduced by illness, defect, or injury to
such an extent that, taking into account age, education, and prior work experience,
they are unable to perform suitable work and earn a reasonable income.

The assessment is always holistic: medical evidence alone is not sufficient.
The adjudicator must consider the claimant's residual functional capacity, age,
education, vocational skills, and the realistic availability of suitable work.

2. WORK CAPACITY THRESHOLD
---------------------------
The statutory incapacity threshold is defined as work capacity reduced to 40% or
less of full capacity (i.e. the claimant can perform at most 40% of the work
output expected for their occupational class).

  - Work capacity <= 40%  : Medical incapacity condition is met.
  - Work capacity 41–59%  : Partial disability pension (osakuntoutustuki) may apply;
                            full pension requires additional justification.
  - Work capacity >= 60%  : Pension cannot be granted on medical grounds alone;
                            vocational rehabilitation must be explored first.

3. MEDICAL EVIDENCE — B-STATEMENT (B-LAAKARINLAUSUNTO)
-------------------------------------------------------
The primary medical evidence document is the B-statement (B-lääkärinlausunto), an
extended specialist medical report prepared by a treating physician. Requirements:

  a. Issued by a qualified specialist physician relevant to the primary diagnosis.
  b. Must contain: diagnosis (with ICD-10 code), symptom description, functional
     limitations, treatment history, rehabilitation measures taken, and prognosis.
  c. Must be dated within 6 months of the claim submission.
  d. Must provide an explicit assessment of work capacity as a percentage or
     qualitative description that can be mapped to a percentage.

A B-statement older than 6 months requires updating before a pension decision
can be made.

4. PROGNOSIS REQUIREMENT
------------------------
The incapacity must be expected to last at least one year. Short-term or
clearly reversible conditions (e.g. post-surgical recovery with strong prognosis)
do not qualify for a permanent pension; a temporary rehabilitation cash benefit
(kuntoutustuki) may apply instead (see DIS-POL-03).

  - Permanent (indefinite): qualifies for full disability pension.
  - Prognosis > 12 months: qualifies for full disability pension.
  - Prognosis 1–12 months: qualifies for kuntoutustuki (temporary), not permanent pension.
  - Prognosis < 1 month:   neither pension nor kuntoutustuki is applicable.

5. CHIEF MEDICAL OFFICER (ASIANTUNTIJALAAKAARI) REFERRAL
---------------------------------------------------------
All cases where the claims handler recommends granting a disability pension must
be referred to the pension institution's Chief Medical Officer (asiantuntijalääkäri,
CMO) for independent medical review before the formal pension decision is issued.

The referral package must include:
  - The original B-statement.
  - A summary of the claimant's occupational and insured earnings history.
  - The qualifying months verification result.
  - A summary of all prior rehabilitation measures (if any).
  - The claims handler's preliminary disposition recommendation.

CMO review is mandatory and cannot be bypassed regardless of how clear the case appears.

6. PARTIAL DISABILITY PENSION
------------------------------
A partial disability pension (osatyökyvyttömyyseläke) may be considered when:
  - Work capacity is reduced to 40–60% range.
  - The claimant is still partially able to work.
  - A workplace accommodation or part-time arrangement is feasible.

Partial pension cases require the same CMO referral and qualifying months check.
