DIS-POL-04 — Qualifying Months Condition and Statutory Exceptions
Source: Finnish Earnings-Related Pension Acts (TyEL § 12; § 66) — statutory summary for internal case handler use.

1. THE 36/60-MONTH QUALIFYING CONDITION (TULEVAN AJAN ANSIOEDELLYTYS)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
To be eligible for a TyEL disability pension with tuleva aika (future-period
accrual), the claimant must have been insured under the Finnish earnings-related
pension system for at least 36 of the 60 calendar months immediately preceding
the onset of incapacity.

  - Insured months >= 36 in the last 60: qualifying condition is MET.
  - Insured months < 36 in the last 60: qualifying condition is NOT MET.
    => The claimant cannot receive a TyEL disability pension with tuleva aika.
    => The claimant should be directed to the Kela national disability pension
       (kansallinen tyokyvyttomyyselake / SOLITA).

The 60-month window is calculated backwards from the date of onset of incapacity
(typically the last day of employment or the accident date).

2. INSURED MONTHS — WHAT COUNTS
---------------------------------
The following periods count toward the 36/60 qualifying window:
  - Active employment with Finnish TyEL/YEL/JuEL coverage.
  - Periods of sick pay (sairausajan palkka) with earnings-related pension deductions.
  - Approved rehabilitation periods under TyEL.
  - Periods covered by a bilateral social security treaty (see section 5 below).

The following do NOT count:
  - Periods of unemployment not covered by TyEL.
  - Uninsured residency abroad (without a bilateral treaty).
  - Self-employment periods without YEL/MYEL registration.
  - Periods of child-raising (kotihoidon tuki) not tied to an insured employment relationship.

3. TULEVA AIKA (FUTURE-PERIOD ACCRUAL)
----------------------------------------
Tuleva aika means the pension institution calculates pension accrual as if the
claimant had continued working at their average earnings until normal retirement age.
This provision significantly increases the pension amount for younger claimants.

Eligibility for tuleva aika requires BOTH:
  a. The 36/60 qualifying months condition is met (or waived under section 4), AND
  b. The claimant's annual earnings in the last full calendar year before incapacity
     exceeded the statutory earnings floor (2025: EUR 8,755).

If either condition fails, tuleva aika does not apply and the pension is calculated
only on actual accrued years.

4. STATUTORY EXCEPTIONS — WHEN THE WAITING PERIOD IS WAIVED
-------------------------------------------------------------
The 36/60-month qualifying condition may be waived in the following circumstances:

  4a. OCCUPATIONAL ACCIDENT (TYOTAPATURMA)
  -----------------------------------------
  If the incapacity arose directly and exclusively from a verified occupational
  accident (tyotapaturma) or an occupational disease (ammattitauti) occurring
  during insured Finnish employment, the waiting period is waived entirely.

  Requirements for the waiver to apply:
    i.   The employer has filed an official accident report with the workers'
         compensation insurance carrier (tapaturmavakuutus).
    ii.  The accident is classified as a tyotapaturma (work-related accident) by
         the workers' compensation insurer or confirmed by an occupational safety
         authority investigation.
    iii. The B-statement links the incapacity directly to the accident injury.
    iv.  The claimant was in active insured employment on the date of the accident.

  Even with only days of insured Finnish employment, a claimant with a verified
  occupational accident can qualify for TyEL disability pension with tuleva aika.

  IMPORTANT: The claims handler must verify accident documentation before
  confirming the waiver. Do NOT assume the waiver applies based on the claimant's
  self-report alone. The employer accident report and insurance classification
  are mandatory evidence.

  4b. YOUNG PERSONS (UNDER 23)
  ------------------------------
  Claimants who became incapacitated before age 23 are eligible for a special
  young persons' provision (nuoren henkilon tyokyvyttomyyselake), which may
  provide a pension even without qualifying months, subject to a separate
  income and incapacity assessment.

  4c. BILATERAL SOCIAL SECURITY TREATIES
  ----------------------------------------
  Finland has bilateral social security treaties with several countries. Insured
  periods in a treaty country may be aggregated with Finnish insured months to
  meet the 36/60 qualifying condition.

  The claims handler must verify whether a treaty applies and obtain the official
  insurance certificate (E101 / A1 form) from the treaty country's authority.

5. WHEN NO EXCEPTION APPLIES — REFERRAL TO KELA
-------------------------------------------------
If the claimant does not meet the 36/60 qualifying condition AND no statutory
exception applies:
  - A TyEL disability pension CANNOT be granted.
  - The case must be closed with a refusal decision under TyEL.
  - The claimant must be informed of their right to apply for the Kela national
    disability pension (tyokyvyttomyysetuus / kansallinen tyokyvyttomyyselake),
    which does not require an insured employment history.
  - Provide the claimant with the Kela contact information and application guidance.
