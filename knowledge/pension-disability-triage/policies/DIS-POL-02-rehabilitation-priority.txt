DIS-POL-02 — Rehabilitation Before Pension (Kuntoutus Ensin)
Source: Finnish Earnings-Related Pension Acts (TyEL § 25; KVTEL § 23) — statutory summary for internal case handler use.

1. THE STATUTORY PRINCIPLE
--------------------------
Finnish law requires that all viable vocational rehabilitation pathways must be
exhausted before a disability pension can be granted to a person of working age.
This is the "Rehabilitation before Pension" principle (kuntoutus ennen eläkettä).

A pension institution is obligated to assess rehabilitation potential proactively
in every disability pension case involving a claimant under the normal retirement age.

2. TRIGGERS FOR REHABILITATION ASSESSMENT
------------------------------------------
A rehabilitation assessment is MANDATORY when ALL of the following apply:

  a. The claimant is under normal retirement age (63 for most TyEL cohorts).
  b. Work capacity is reduced but not completely absent (typically 40–70%).
  c. The primary diagnosis or condition does not completely preclude all forms of work.
  d. The claimant has not previously completed a structured vocational rehabilitation
     programme that addressed the current condition.

When all four conditions are met, the claims handler must recommend rehabilitation
as the primary disposition and MUST NOT proceed to pension evaluation.

3. VOCATIONAL REHABILITATION OPTIONS
--------------------------------------
The following rehabilitation measures are available under TyEL:

  a. Workplace trial (Tyokokeilu / arbetsproving):
     A structured, time-limited trial at the claimant's current or a new employer
     in modified or alternative duties. Duration: typically 1–3 months.
     The claimant receives a rehabilitation allowance during the trial.

  b. Vocational retraining (Ammatillinen koulutus):
     Subsidised retraining for a new vocation or role that the claimant can perform
     given their remaining functional capacity. Duration: up to 3 years.
     Pension institution finances the training and provides a living allowance.

  c. Work ability support (Tyokyvyn tuki / tyokykyneuvonta):
     Early-stage counselling and coordination with the employer and occupational
     health service to prevent incapacity from escalating. Not a pension measure.

  d. Supported employment and assistive equipment:
     Subsidised workplace ergonomic adaptations, assistive technology, or supported
     employment arrangements.

4. EMPLOYER DUTY TO ACCOMMODATE
---------------------------------
Before a rehabilitation or pension decision is finalised, the employer must provide
a written report confirming:
  - What workplace accommodation measures were explored and implemented.
  - Whether modified or alternative roles are available within the organisation.
  - The outcome of any prior workplace trials.

If the employer's duty-to-accommodate report is absent or incomplete, the claims
handler must request it before making a disposition.

5. WHEN REHABILITATION CAN BE WAIVED
--------------------------------------
Rehabilitation assessment may be skipped only if ONE of the following applies:

  a. The claimant's condition is permanent and irreversible, and all prior
     rehabilitation measures (including workplace trials and retraining) have
     already been formally attempted and documented as exhausted.
  b. The claimant's work capacity is so severely reduced (well below 40%) that
     no form of work is realistically possible, and this is confirmed by the CMO.
  c. The claimant is approaching normal retirement age (typically within 2 years)
     and the rehabilitation horizon would be shorter than the benefit period.
  d. A verified occupational accident (see DIS-POL-04) has caused severe permanent
     injury that makes any vocational activity impossible.

6. COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID
-----------------------------
  - Do NOT grant a pension to a working-age claimant with significant recovery
    potential simply because the B-statement mentions reduced work capacity.
  - Do NOT skip the workplace trial step because the claimant has not yet attempted one.
  - Do NOT treat the absence of an employer accommodation report as evidence that
    accommodation was attempted and failed; request the report explicitly.
