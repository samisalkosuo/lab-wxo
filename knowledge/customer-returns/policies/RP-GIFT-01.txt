RP-GIFT-01: Gift Returns

SYMPTOMS
- Customer says the product was a gift and they do not have an order confirmation or order ID.
- Customer provides only the product itself or its serial number.
- Customer uses phrases such as "I received this as a gift", "I don't have the receipt",
  "it was given to me".

POLICY
Gift recipients may return a product without an order ID by providing the product serial number.
The serial number is printed on the product packaging and on the device label.

ELIGIBILITY
A gift return is eligible when ALL of the following are true:
1. The serial number is found in the product registry and links to an original order.
2. The original purchase date (from the linked order) is within 30 calendar days.
3. No prior return has been filed for the linked order.

PROCEDURE
1. Ask the customer for the product serial number.
2. Use the serial-number lookup tool to find the linked order and purchase date.
3. Check that the purchase date is within the 30-day gift-return window.
4. Check the return history for the linked order to confirm no duplicate return.
5. If eligible, the outcome is store credit (preferred for gift returns) or a replacement.
   Full cash refund is not available for gift returns because the original payment method
   is not associated with the recipient.
6. Route the approved outcome through the returns_process_return flow for human sign-off.

OUTCOME
- Eligible gift return: store credit or replacement.
- Denied (outside 30 days): no return; advise customer of policy.
