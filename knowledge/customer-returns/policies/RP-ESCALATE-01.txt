RP-ESCALATE-01: Out-of-Scope Requests and Escalation

SYMPTOMS
- Customer reports a billing error, duplicate charge, or payment discrepancy.
- Customer requests a chargeback or disputes a transaction.
- Customer asks about delivery delays or missing parcels (not a return).
- Customer asks about warranty claims that involve a third-party manufacturer directly.
- The request does not fit the standard return, warranty, or gift-return categories.

POLICY
The returns agent handles product returns, warranty replacements, and gift returns only.
Billing disputes, duplicate charges, delivery issues, and manufacturer claims are handled
by specialised teams and must be escalated.

PROCEDURE
1. Confirm that the issue does not fit RP-WARRANTY-01, RP-STANDARD-01, or RP-GIFT-01.
2. Acknowledge the customer's concern clearly and politely.
3. Inform the customer that this request is outside the scope of the returns process.
4. Direct the customer to the appropriate channel:
   - Billing disputes and duplicate charges: contact the Billing team via the account portal
     or by calling the billing support line.
   - Delivery issues and missing parcels: contact the Logistics team with the order ID.
   - Manufacturer warranty (direct): refer to the manufacturer's support contact in the
     product documentation.
5. Do NOT attempt to process a refund or return for an out-of-scope request.
6. Do NOT speculate about outcomes or timelines for the specialist teams.

OUTCOME
- Agent clearly declines to process the return and directs customer to the correct channel.
- No flow is triggered for out-of-scope escalations.
