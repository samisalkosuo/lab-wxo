RP-WARRANTY-01: Warranty Returns and Replacements

SYMPTOMS
- Customer reports the product stopped working, broke, or is defective.
- Customer says the item failed within the warranty period.
- Customer uses phrases such as "it just broke", "it stopped working", "it is defective".

POLICY
Products sold by this store carry a standard 1-year manufacturer warranty from the date of purchase.

ELIGIBILITY
A warranty return or replacement is eligible when ALL of the following are true:
1. The product has a warranty record on file linked to the specific order and SKU.
2. The warranty is currently active (not expired as of the return request date).
3. The reported fault is covered by the warranty (manufacturing defects, mechanical failure).
   Accidental damage, cosmetic damage, and user-caused wear are NOT covered.

IMPORTANT: Verify that the warranty record matches the exact order and SKU the customer is
returning. A customer may have multiple products; a warranty for a different SKU does not
cover a different product, even if both products are from the same brand.

PROCEDURE
1. Look up the order with the order ID to confirm the product and customer.
2. Check the warranty record for that specific order.
3. If the warranty is active and the fault is covered, the eligible outcome is a replacement
   (preferred) or full refund at the customer's choice.
4. If the warranty has expired or does not cover the reported fault, proceed to RP-STANDARD-01
   to check the no-reason return window instead.
5. Route the approved outcome through the returns_process_return flow for human sign-off.

OUTCOME
- Approved warranty claim: replacement unit or full refund.
- Denied (expired or not covered): inform customer of denial reason; refer to RP-STANDARD-01.
