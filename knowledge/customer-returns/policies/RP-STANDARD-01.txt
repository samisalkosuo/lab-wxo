RP-STANDARD-01: Standard No-Reason Return Window

SYMPTOMS
- Customer says they changed their mind, no longer want the product, or it does not suit them.
- Customer provides no specific defect or fault.
- Customer uses phrases such as "I just don't like it", "I changed my mind", "it is not for me".

POLICY
Customers may return any product for any reason (no reason required) within 30 calendar days
of the original purchase date, provided the product is in resalable condition.

ELIGIBILITY
A standard no-reason return is eligible when ALL of the following are true:
1. The order is found in the system and belongs to the requesting customer.
2. The purchase date is within the last 30 calendar days (inclusive).
3. The product has not already been returned (check return history).
4. The purchase channel is 'online' or 'in_store'. Gift purchases use RP-GIFT-01.

NOT ELIGIBLE
- Orders older than 30 days from the purchase date are outside the standard return window.
- If the product is defective, use RP-WARRANTY-01 instead (warranty may extend eligibility).

PROCEDURE
1. Look up the order with the order ID.
2. Check the order age in days against the 30-day window.
3. Check the return history to confirm no prior return has been filed.
4. If eligible, the outcome is a full refund to the original payment method.
5. Route the approved outcome through the returns_process_return flow for human sign-off.
6. If the order is outside the 30-day window and there is no warranty claim, the return
   is denied. Inform the customer clearly and politely, citing this policy document (RP-STANDARD-01).

OUTCOME
- Eligible (within 30 days): full refund.
- Denied (outside 30 days, no warranty): no refund; advise customer of policy.
