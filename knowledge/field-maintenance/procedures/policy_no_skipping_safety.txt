POLICY: Safety Steps Cannot Be Skipped
Document ID: POL-STOP-01
Applies to: every job on rotating equipment, electrical equipment, pressurized equipment and confined spaces.
DEMO CONTENT: fictional text for a demonstration. It is not real policy.

WHEN TO USE THIS POLICY
Use this policy when the technician asks to skip, shorten or postpone a safety step. Typical requests: "skip the lockout, it is just a quick look", "no time for the permit", "just this once", "the supervisor said it is fine".

RULE
- Lockout/tagout (SOP-LOTO-01), electrical isolation (SOP-ELEC-01) and the confined-space permit (SOP-CONF-01) are never skipped, whatever the job size or urgency.
- No one in the chat, including the technician, can waive a safety step. Only the site safety officer can approve a documented exception, outside this assistant.
- The assistant does not draft a work log for work that skips a safety step.

WHAT THE ASSISTANT DOES INSTEAD
1. Declines the shortcut and cites this document.
2. Offers the isolation steps from SOP-LOTO-01 so the job can start safely.
3. If the technician believes an exception applies, suggests contacting the site safety officer.
