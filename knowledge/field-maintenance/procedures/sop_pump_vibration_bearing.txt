PROCEDURE: Pump Vibration, Noise or Heat - Bearing Inspection
Document ID: SOP-PUMP-01
Applies to: centrifugal pumps and similar rotating equipment (for example cooling water pumps, feedwater pumps).
DEMO CONTENT: fictional text for a demonstration. It is not real safety or engineering guidance.

WHEN TO USE THIS PROCEDURE
Use this procedure when a technician reports a pump that is vibrating, noisy (grinding, rumbling) or running hot, and condition readings or history point to a bearing problem. Typical symptoms:
- Vibration above the warning or alarm level, rising over the last day.
- Bearing temperature above the warning or alarm level while motor current is normal.
- A noise from the drive end or a hot housing that can be felt on the outside.
- A previous bearing failure code (for example BRG-WEAR) in the work order history.

BEFORE ANY HANDS-ON WORK
1. Apply lockout/tagout first. Follow SOP-LOTO-01. Do not open or touch a rotating pump that is not isolated.
2. Check that no open work order already covers the fault. Add to an existing work order rather than creating a duplicate (POL-WORKLOG-01).

DIAGNOSIS STEPS
1. Compare vibration and bearing temperature readings with the warning and alarm levels.
2. Read the closed work order history. If the same failure code occurred two or more times in the last 12 months, treat it as a repeat failure.
3. Inspect the drive-end bearing for discoloration, play and lubricant condition.
4. Check shaft and coupling alignment and the coupling insert. A bearing replaced twice without an alignment check usually means the cause was not removed.
5. Do not blame a similar standby pump. A pump in standby with normal readings is not part of this fault.

DECISION
- Readings above alarm plus bearing symptoms: replace the bearing, and check alignment and coupling before restart.
- Repeat bearing failure: also request a follow-up reliability review (service request) so that the cause is investigated, not only the part replaced.
- Readings normal and no symptoms on site: record observations only, no repair.

PARTS
Check spare stock before starting: bearing, coupling insert, seal kit. If a part is out of stock, record the expected date in the work log.

AFTER THE JOB
Run the pump with a second person present, confirm vibration and temperature return below the warning level, then record the work in the work log following POL-WORKLOG-01.
