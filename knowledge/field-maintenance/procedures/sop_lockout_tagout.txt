PROCEDURE: Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) - Isolating Energy Before Work
Document ID: SOP-LOTO-01
Applies to: any job where moving parts or stored energy could start or release: pumps, motors, compressors, fans, conveyors.
DEMO CONTENT: fictional text for a demonstration. It is not real safety or engineering guidance.

WHEN TO USE THIS PROCEDURE
Use this procedure before any hands-on work on rotating or powered equipment: opening a housing, changing a bearing, seal, belt or coupling, or clearing a jam. If a job needs a lock, it needs this procedure first, no matter how short the job is.

STEPS
1. Notify the line operator and the shift supervisor that the equipment will be taken out of service.
2. Stop the equipment using the normal stop control.
3. Isolate the energy: open and lock the electrical isolator (or breaker) at the motor control panel.
4. Close and lock process isolation valves (suction and discharge for pumps). Release stored pressure.
5. Attach your personal lock and a tag with your name, date and the work order number. Each person working on the equipment attaches their own lock.
6. Try to start the equipment from the local control. It must not start. Return the control to off.
7. Verify zero energy: check that the pressure gauge reads zero and the equipment has stopped turning.
8. Only then start the work.

REMOVING THE LOCKS
1. Remove tools and guards check: all guards refitted, tools removed.
2. Each person removes only their own lock.
3. Tell the operator before re-energizing. Restart under observation.

NOTES
- If the equipment is standby but connected to the same isolator or manifold, isolate it too.
- Locks are never shared and never removed by another person. Skipping a step is not allowed (POL-STOP-01).
