ARTICLE ID: HR-TK-ART-05
TITLE: Internal Position Change Request Process — Sweden
STATUS: current
OWNER ROLE: HR Specialist
LAST VERIFIED: 2025-01-08
APPLIES TO: Sweden HR Coordinators and managers

SYMPTOMS
- A manager or employee in Sweden asks how to request an internal position change.
- A coordinator in Sweden receives a position-change request and is unsure of the process.
- A Sweden-based employee asks how long a position change takes to process.

PROCEDURE
1. OPEN THE HR PORTAL.
   Log into the HR portal (HR > Requests > Position Change). Use the Sweden HR portal instance.

2. COMPLETE THE SWEDEN POSITION-CHANGE FORM.
   Fill in the employee name, current position, requested new position, effective date,
   and the manager confirmation.

3. SUBMIT THE REQUEST.
   Submit the completed form in the HR portal.

4. WAIT FOR PROCESSING.
   Sweden HR processes internal position change requests within 7 working days.

5. CONFIRM THE CHANGE.
   HR will send a confirmation to the manager and the employee by email.

OUTCOME
- The internal position change is processed within 7 working days in Sweden.
- Both the manager and the employee receive a confirmation email.
- The HR record is updated with the new position and effective date.

NOTE: This article covers the Sweden process only. For Finland, see HR-TK-ART-04.
The processing time and form differ between countries.
