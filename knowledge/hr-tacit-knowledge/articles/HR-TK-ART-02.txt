ARTICLE ID: HR-TK-ART-02
TITLE: Requesting an Employment Certificate (Legacy Process — Superseded)
STATUS: superseded
OWNER ROLE: HR Operations
LAST VERIFIED: 2023-06-01
APPLIES TO: Historical reference only — superseded by HR-TK-ART-03

NOTE: This article describes the old paper-form process, which was replaced in 2024.
Do not follow this procedure. Use HR-TK-ART-03 instead.

SYMPTOMS
(Historical — this article is superseded)
- Employee needs a proof-of-employment letter.
- HR Coordinator receives a request for an employment certificate.

PROCEDURE (LEGACY — DO NOT USE)
The old process required:
1. Complete paper form HR-CERT-2022.
2. Submit to HR Administration in person or by internal mail.
3. HR Administration processes within 5 working days.
4. Certificate delivered by internal mail.

This process was replaced when the Employee Self-Service portal was introduced in 2024.

OUTCOME
(Historical) Certificate issued via paper form and internal mail.
