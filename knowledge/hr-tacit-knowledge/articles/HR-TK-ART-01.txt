ARTICLE ID: HR-TK-ART-01
TITLE: Correcting a Retroactive Leave Start Date Without Triggering Overpayment Recovery
STATUS: current
OWNER ROLE: Senior HR Specialist
LAST VERIFIED: 2024-11-15
APPLIES TO: All Finland HR Specialists
NOTE: The payroll cutoff date in this article (18th of the month) is STALE. The correct
cutoff since the January 2025 system migration is the 22nd. This article is scheduled for
update. Use the 22nd in all communications until this article is revised.

SYMPTOMS
- A manager requests a retroactive change to an employee leave start date.
- The system sends an automatic overpayment-recovery letter before the correction is confirmed.
- The employee receives a recovery letter and is distressed before HR has finished the correction.
- The HR Specialist needs to correct a leave start date without triggering the automatic letter.

PROCEDURE
1. OPEN THE PAYROLL HOLD FIRST.
   Before making any date change, contact the payroll team to place a manual hold on the
   salary component for the affected employee. This prevents the automatic
   overpayment-recovery calculation from running during the correction window.
   Note: payroll team contact details are on the HR Operations intranet page.

2. CHANGE THE LEAVE START DATE.
   With the hold in place, update the leave start date in the HR system. The system
   calculates the correction silently, without triggering the recovery letter.

3. REVIEW THE RECALCULATION.
   The overnight batch recalculates the amounts. Check the result the next morning.
   If the amount looks correct, proceed to step 4.

4. AGREE ON A REPAYMENT SCHEDULE (IF ANY).
   If a repayment is owed, agree on the schedule with the employee.
   - Up to 3 periods: no manager approval needed; the HR Specialist can confirm.
   - 4 or more periods: written approval from the line manager is required.
   Document the agreement in the case notes with the date and the employee verbal consent.

5. RELEASE THE HOLD AND CALL THE EMPLOYEE.
   Release the payroll hold after the schedule is agreed. Call the employee before any
   letter is generated. The system sends the recovery letter automatically once the hold
   is released, so the employee must be prepared before the letter arrives.

PAYROLL CUTOFF
The intranet page on payroll corrections still states the cutoff as the 18th of the month.
This is stale: since the January 2025 payroll system migration, the cutoff is the 22nd.
Corrections submitted after the 22nd fall into the following month run.
(Planned update pending HR Content team action.)

OUTCOME
- The retroactive leave start date is corrected without an unexpected recovery letter.
- The employee is informed before any letter arrives.
- The repayment schedule is documented in the case notes.
