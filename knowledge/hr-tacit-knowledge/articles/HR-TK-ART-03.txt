ARTICLE ID: HR-TK-ART-03
TITLE: Requesting an Employment Certificate via Employee Self-Service
STATUS: current
OWNER ROLE: HR Operations Lead
LAST VERIFIED: 2025-03-10
APPLIES TO: All employees in Finland

SYMPTOMS
- An employee needs a proof-of-employment letter (for a bank, landlord, authority, or other party).
- A new hire asks how to get an employment certificate.
- A coordinator is asked to help an employee obtain official confirmation of employment status.
- An employee needs to confirm job title, start date, or employment type in writing.

PROCEDURE
1. LOG INTO EMPLOYEE SELF-SERVICE.
   Open Employee Self-Service (ESS) at the link on the HR intranet homepage.

2. NAVIGATE TO MY DOCUMENTS > CERTIFICATES.
   From the ESS menu, select My Documents, then Certificates.

3. SELECT EMPLOYMENT CERTIFICATE.
   Choose Employment Certificate from the certificate type list.

4. SELECT THE LANGUAGE.
   Choose Finnish, English, or Swedish.

5. ADD A NOTE IF NEEDED.
   If the recipient needs a specific reference (for example a bank loan letter or
   a rental application), add a note in the free-text field. HR Operations will
   contact the employee if further information is needed.

6. SUBMIT THE REQUEST.
   Click Submit. The request goes directly to HR Operations for processing.

7. WAIT FOR PROCESSING.
   HR Operations processes employment certificate requests within 3 working days.
   The certificate is delivered electronically via the ESS portal.

OUTCOME
- Employee receives an official employment certificate in the requested language within 3 working days.
- Certificate includes job title, start date, and employment type.
- Additional details (salary, specific references) are included only if the employee requested them.
