ARTICLE ID: HR-TK-ART-04
TITLE: Internal Position Change Request Process — Finland
STATUS: current
OWNER ROLE: HR Specialist
LAST VERIFIED: 2024-08-20
APPLIES TO: Finland HR Coordinators and managers
NOTE: The form and processing time in this article are STALE. The correct procedure since
January 2025 uses e-form PR-1 (not form HR-22) and takes 5 working days (not 10).
This article is scheduled for update. Until revised, use e-form PR-1 and 5 working days.

SYMPTOMS
- A manager or employee asks how to request an internal position change in Finland.
- A coordinator receives a position-change request and is unsure which form to use.
- An employee asks how long a position change takes to process.
- A position-change request was submitted on the old paper form HR-22 and was rejected.

PROCEDURE
1. OPEN THE HR PORTAL.
   Log into the HR portal (HR > Requests > Position Change).
   Note: use e-form PR-1 (the old paper form HR-22 was retired in January 2025).

2. COMPLETE E-FORM PR-1.
   Fill in the employee name, current position, requested new position, effective date,
   and the manager confirmation. Attach any supporting documentation.

3. SUBMIT THE REQUEST.
   Submit the completed e-form PR-1 in the HR portal.

4. WAIT FOR PROCESSING.
   HR processes internal position change requests within 5 working days.
   (The intranet page still shows 10 working days — this is stale. Use 5 working days.)

5. CONFIRM THE CHANGE.
   HR will confirm the change by email to the manager and the employee.

OUTCOME
- The internal position change is processed within 5 working days.
- Both the manager and the employee receive a confirmation email.
- The HR record is updated to reflect the new position and effective date.
