YEL-POL-02: Triennial Review Cycle and Transition Adjustment Cap
================================================================

Document ID:  YEL-POL-02
Scope:        Finnish Self-Employed Persons' Pensions Act (YEL), 2023 reform
Applicability: All established YEL policyholders subject to triennial review


1. BACKGROUND — THE 2023 YEL REFORM

The 2023 YEL reform (HE 82/2022 vp, effective 1 January 2023) made two major
changes relevant to income reviews:

  a) Mandatory triennial (3-year) review cycle:
     Pension providers are now required to review every policyholder's confirmed
     work income every three years against current sector wage benchmarks.
     Previously, reviews were largely voluntary and driven by the entrepreneur.

  b) Statutory transition adjustment cap (korotuskatto):
     To avoid sudden premium shocks for entrepreneurs whose income had long been
     set below the sector benchmark, a maximum mandatory increase per review cycle
     was introduced. The cap for 2024/2025 reviews is EUR 4,000 per year per
     review period (korotuskatto 4 000 EUR/vuosi).


2. HOW THE TRANSITION CAP WORKS

When the sector-based recommended income exceeds the current confirmed income:

  Case A — Proposed increase <= EUR 4,000/yr:
    The full increase may be applied immediately.
    Example: Current income EUR 20,000; sector target EUR 23,000.
    Increase = EUR 3,000 <= cap. New confirmed income = EUR 23,000.

  Case B — Proposed increase > EUR 4,000/yr:
    Only EUR 4,000 increase is MANDATORY at this review.
    The remainder may be applied at the next triennial review (or voluntarily).
    Example: Current income EUR 18,000; sector target EUR 46,000.
    Proposed increase = EUR 28,000 > cap.
    Mandatory new income = EUR 18,000 + EUR 4,000 = EUR 22,000.
    Remaining EUR 24,000 gap is addressed in future reviews.

  Case C — Current income >= sector target:
    No mandatory increase. No mandatory reduction.
    The entrepreneur may voluntarily request a reduction if their business has
    changed significantly, subject to YEL-POL-01 minimum threshold rules.


3. VOLUNTARY ADJUSTMENTS

Entrepreneurs may always request a voluntary upward adjustment of their confirmed
income between triennial review dates, for example when the business grows rapidly
or when a new social security event (illness, parental leave) is anticipated.

Voluntary downward adjustments are possible but must be substantiated (e.g. reduced
working hours, documented business contraction). The confirmed income must not fall
below the statutory minimum (EUR 9,010/yr in 2024/2025).


4. REVIEW NOTIFICATION AND TIMING

  - The pension provider initiates the review by notifying the entrepreneur at least
    30 days before the proposed effective date.
  - The entrepreneur has the right to respond (kuuleminen, see YEL-POL-04).
  - The confirmed income change takes effect on the first day of the month following
    the decision date unless the underwriter specifies otherwise.


5. UNDERWRITER WORKFLOW FOR TRIENNIAL REVIEW

  Step 1: Retrieve current confirmed income and sector benchmark data.
  Step 2: Calculate the uncapped recommended income.
  Step 3: Apply the EUR 4,000 cap if the proposed increase exceeds it.
  Step 4: Prepare the formal proposal for the entrepreneur's review (kuuleminen).
  Step 5: If the entrepreneur disputes the proposal, assess their evidence
          (YEL-POL-04) and adjust if substantiated.
  Step 6: Issue the formal administrative determination (valituskelpoinen päätös).
