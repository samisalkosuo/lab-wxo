YEL-POL-01: Confirmed Work Income — Definition and Principles
=============================================================

Document ID:  YEL-POL-01
Scope:        Finnish Self-Employed Persons' Pensions Act (YEL, 1272/2006)
Applicability: All self-employed persons (yrittäjät) subject to YEL insurance


1. WHAT IS YEL CONFIRMED WORK INCOME (VAHVISTETTU TYÖTULO)?

YEL confirmed work income is not the entrepreneur's turnover, profit, or actual
drawings. It is the statutory estimate of the annual wage that would be paid for
the same work if performed by an employee in the open labour market. This "wage
replacement" principle (palkankorvaamisperiaate) is the legal basis for setting
the income level.

Key points:
  - Turnover (liikevaihto) is evidence of business activity but is NOT the income
    figure used for YEL premium calculation.
  - Dividends, capital income, and partner drawings are excluded.
  - The relevant question is: "What would a comparable employee earn for the same
    work input?"
  - The sector median wage from Statistics Finland and ETK (Finnish Centre for
    Pensions) sector wage indices is the primary benchmark.

2. WHEN IS YEL INSURANCE MANDATORY?

YEL is mandatory when all three conditions are met:
  a) The person is self-employed (yrittäjä) in Finland.
  b) The annual self-employment income (work contribution value) equals or exceeds
     the statutory minimum threshold (9,010 EUR/yr in 2024/2025).
  c) The person is between 18 and 68 years of age.

If annual revenue is below 9,010 EUR/yr, YEL is NOT mandatory. Voluntary insurance
is available but is not required.

3. INCOME ASSESSMENT METHOD

Step 1: Identify the entrepreneur's primary NACE sector.
Step 2: Retrieve the Statistics Finland / ETK sector median wage for that sector.
Step 3: Adjust for actual weekly working hours (proportional to full-time 40 h/wk).
Step 4: Consider personal factors (specialisation, experience, market position).
Step 5: The final confirmed income must fall between the statutory minimum
        (9,010 EUR/yr) and the statutory maximum (204,625 EUR/yr) for 2024/2025.

4. EVIDENCE THE ENTREPRENEUR MAY SUBMIT

Entrepreneurs have the right to present evidence (kuuleminen, see YEL-POL-04):
  - Tax returns (veroilmoitus) showing revenue and expenses.
  - Time records (tuntikirjanpito) documenting weekly working hours.
  - Invoices for subcontracting or purchased services (alihankintakulut).
  - Industry association wage surveys.

The underwriter must consider all submitted evidence before issuing a determination.

5. UNDERWRITER OBLIGATION

The underwriter (YEL-ratkaisija) must:
  - Document the income assessment reasoning in the case file.
  - Cite the sector benchmark data source (e.g. "ETK / Statistics Finland 2024, J.62").
  - Issue a legally appealable administrative decision (valituskelpoinen päätös).
  - Notify the entrepreneur in writing within 5 business days.
