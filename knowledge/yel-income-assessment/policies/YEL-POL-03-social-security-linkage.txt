YEL-POL-03: Social Security Linkage and Beginner Discount
==========================================================

Document ID:  YEL-POL-03
Scope:        Finnish Self-Employed Persons' Pensions Act (YEL) — benefit linkage
Applicability: All YEL policyholders; beginner discount for first-time entrepreneurs


1. WHY THE YEL CONFIRMED INCOME LEVEL MATTERS BEYOND PENSION

The YEL confirmed work income is the basis not only for the YEL premium and future
pension, but also for most Kela (Social Insurance Institution of Finland) income-
replacement benefits and for earnings-related unemployment security. Setting the
income too low directly reduces the entrepreneur's safety net.

Key benefits linked to YEL confirmed income:

  a) Sickness allowance (sairauspäiväraha):
     Kela calculates the daily sickness allowance as approximately 70 % of the annual
     YEL income divided by 300 working days. A low YEL income means a low daily
     allowance during illness.

  b) Parental allowance (vanhempainpäiväraha):
     The same YEL income base determines the daily parental allowance for maternity
     and paternity leave. An entrepreneur planning a family benefits substantially
     from a realistic YEL income.

  c) Earnings-related unemployment security (ansiosidonnainen työttömyysturva):
     Self-employed persons who are members of an unemployment fund for entrepreneurs
     (e.g. SYT-kassa, AYT-kassa) receive a daily unemployment allowance based on
     their YEL income. The earnings-related component is approximately 45 % of the
     daily YEL wage above a threshold of EUR 119/day (illustrative 2024 figure).

  d) Pension accrual (eläkekertymä):
     Pension accrues at 1.5 % of YEL income per year for ages under 53, and at
     1.9 % per year for ages 53–62, in line with TyEL/YEL rules.

2. ILLUSTRATIVE COMPARISON: LOW vs. REALISTIC YEL INCOME

Example — Aleksi Mäkinen, IT consultant (ENT-801):
  Current YEL income:    EUR 18,000/yr
    Daily sick allowance: EUR 42/day
  Recommended income:    EUR 22,000/yr
    Daily sick allowance: EUR 51.33/day  (+EUR 9.33/day, +22 %)

This means that for a 30-day illness, a realistic YEL income would add approximately
EUR 280 in benefit payments. For parental leave (160 working days), the difference
is approximately EUR 1,493. These are illustrative figures; actual Kela calculations
apply income ceilings and adjustment rules not modelled here.


3. BEGINNER DISCOUNT (ALOITTAVAN YRITTAJAN ALENNUS)

First-time entrepreneurs who have not previously been YEL-insured are entitled to a
22 % discount on their YEL premium for the first 48 months of insurance.

Rules:
  - Entitlement period: the first 48 consecutive months of YEL insurance.
  - Discount applies to the premium only; it does NOT reduce the confirmed income
    or the benefit base.
  - The discount is applied automatically by the pension provider.
  - If the entrepreneur has had a prior YEL period (even briefly), the beginner
    discount does not restart.

Example — Juhani Korhonen, startup founder (ENT-803), 19 months insured:
  Confirmed income:    EUR 18,000/yr (after cap, per YEL-POL-02)
  Standard premium rate: approx. 24.1 % = EUR 362.50/month
  Beginner discount 22 %: EUR 79.75/month reduction
  Discounted premium:    EUR 282.75/month

Note: The 22 % discount does not affect the social security benefit calculation.
The full income of EUR 18,000/yr is still used for sickness and parental allowances.


4. UNDERWRITER GUIDANCE

  - Always present the social security benefit impact alongside the premium change.
  - Entrepreneurs often focus on the higher premium; showing the benefit improvement
    helps contextualise the increase.
  - For beginner entrepreneurs, confirm the discount entitlement period and end date.
  - Cite this document (YEL-POL-03) when explaining benefit linkage in the decision.
