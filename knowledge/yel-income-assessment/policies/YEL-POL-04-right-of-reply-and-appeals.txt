YEL-POL-04: Right of Reply and Appeals Process
===============================================

Document ID:  YEL-POL-04
Scope:        Finnish Self-Employed Persons' Pensions Act (YEL) — administrative procedure
Applicability: All YEL policyholders subject to a proposed income determination


1. RIGHT OF REPLY (KUULEMINEN)

Before the pension provider issues a formal income determination that changes the
entrepreneur's confirmed work income, the entrepreneur has the statutory right to be
heard (kuuleminen, Administrative Procedure Act 434/2003 and YEL Act).

Procedure:
  1. The pension provider sends the entrepreneur a written proposal (kuulemiskirje)
     stating the proposed new confirmed income and the reasons.
  2. The entrepreneur has at least 14 days to respond.
  3. The entrepreneur may accept the proposal, dispute it, or request a meeting.
  4. Acceptable evidence for disputing a proposal includes:
       - Most recent tax return (veroilmoitus).
       - Time records or diary (tuntikirjanpito) documenting actual weekly hours.
       - Invoices for subcontracting costs or purchased services (alihankintakulut).
       - Industry or sector wage surveys (e.g. from a professional association).
       - Medical certificates if reduced working capacity affects work input.
  5. The underwriter reviews all submitted evidence and adjusts the recommendation
     if the evidence substantiates a different income level.
  6. The formal determination (valituskelpoinen päätös) is issued after kuuleminen
     is complete.


2. FORMAL ADMINISTRATIVE DETERMINATION (VALITUSKELPOINEN PAATOS)

The income determination is a legally binding administrative decision that must:
  - State the new confirmed YEL work income.
  - Include the effective date (first day of a calendar month).
  - Provide the reasoning (perustelut), including the sector benchmark cited.
  - State the appeal right and deadline (30 days from receipt).
  - Be issued in writing and delivered to the entrepreneur.


3. APPEAL PROCESS (MUUTOKSENHAKU)

If the entrepreneur disagrees with the formal determination, they may appeal:

  a) Internal rectification request (oikaisuvaatimus):
     First step: submit a written rectification request to the pension provider
     within 30 days of receiving the decision. The provider must respond in writing.

  b) Appeal to TELK (Tyoelakelakiasioiden muutoksenhakulautakunta):
     If the rectification request is rejected or the entrepreneur disagrees with the
     outcome, they may appeal to TELK (Pension Appeal Board for Earnings-Related
     Pension Acts) within 30 days of receiving the rectification decision.
     TELK rulings are binding and set precedent.

  c) Further appeal to the Insurance Court (Vakuutusoikeus):
     TELK decisions may be appealed to the Insurance Court on legal grounds only.

  d) Supreme Court (Korkein oikeus):
     Further appeal to the Supreme Court requires a leave to appeal and is limited
     to questions of law.

Escalation flag in the system:
  When an underwriter escalates a case to appeals review, the case is logged as
  ESCALATE. No income change takes effect while the appeal is pending. The
  entrepreneur is notified by post within 5 business days of escalation.


4. DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS FOR UNDERWRITERS

Underwriters must retain in the case file:
  - The kuulemiskirje and the entrepreneur's response (or note of no response).
  - All evidence submitted by the entrepreneur.
  - The written reasoning for the final income level chosen.
  - The formal determination letter and delivery confirmation.

Retention period: 10 years from the date of the determination.


5. SPECIAL CASES: CONTESTED INCOME DISPUTES

If the entrepreneur presents credible evidence that their actual work input is
substantially less than the sector median suggests (e.g. documented part-time
working hours, high subcontracting costs that reduce owner contribution), the
underwriter must adjust the recommendation accordingly and document the adjustment.

Example — Kaisa Virtanen, artisan bakery (ENT-802):
  Proposed income:     EUR 38,000/yr (based on turnover comparison)
  Disputed: owner works only 20 h/wk; 3 staff handle production.
  Evidence provided:   payroll records for 3 employees; owner's diary showing 20 h/wk.
  Adjusted target:     Sector median (EUR 32,000) * 20/40 = EUR 16,000.
  However, current income is EUR 32,000 (above adjusted target); no increase mandatory.
  Underwriter must explain why the proposal was incorrect and document the correction.
