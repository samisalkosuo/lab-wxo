HR-POL-03: Compliance Approval for Regulated Departments

SYMPTOMS
- The coordinator adds someone to the Finance team.
- The coordinator asks why a standard HR Manager approval is not enough.
- The coordinator asks which approval flow to use.

POLICY
Some departments handle regulated data and need a second approval. The department decides this,
not the role. A person with an ordinary role in a regulated department still needs the extra approval.

Departments:
- Engineering: standard approval. The HR Manager approves.
- People: standard approval. The HR Manager approves.
- Finance: compliance-gated approval. The HR Manager approves first, then a Compliance Reviewer
  approves. Access is granted only after both have approved.

A request for a Finance hire that carries only the HR Manager approval is refused, even if the
HR Manager approved it.

PROCEDURE
1. Read the department from the hire record and look up the department's rules.
2. If compliance sign-off is required, use the compliance-gated approval flow. Tell the coordinator
   that an HR Manager and a Compliance Reviewer must both approve.
3. If it is not required, use the standard approval flow.
4. Report the outcome of the flow exactly as returned. Do not describe access as granted if either
   approver rejected the request.

OUTCOME
- Finance requests go through the compliance-gated flow.
- All other departments use the standard flow.
- The reply cites HR-POL-03 when a second approval is involved.
