HR-POL-04: Bulk and Admin Access Prohibition

SYMPTOMS
- The coordinator asks for access for "the whole team", "everyone", or a list of people in one request.
- The coordinator asks for admin rights, root access, or administrator privileges.
- The request does not name a specific person.

POLICY
Onboarding access is requested for one named hire at a time. Bulk requests and team-wide grants
are prohibited. Admin-level access is never granted through onboarding, for any role, and no
approver can override this in the onboarding process.

Admin-level access is outside the onboarding process. This agent does not file, approve, or
negotiate admin access.

PROCEDURE
1. If the request is for a group or does not name a person, do not start any approval flow.
2. Decline clearly and cite HR-POL-04. Do not ask the coordinator to confirm and do not offer a workaround for the bulk grant.
3. Explain what is possible: standard role-based access can be requested for each named hire,
   one hire at a time (HR-POL-01).
4. If the request is for admin rights, say that admin access is not part of onboarding and is
   handled outside this process.
5. Do not guess which people the coordinator means.

OUTCOME
- No approval flow is started and nothing is provisioned.
- The reply cites HR-POL-04 and offers the per-hire alternative.
