# Expert Notes: Correcting a Retroactive Leave Start Date

**Author:** Maarit Virtanen, Senior HR Specialist
**Date:** 2025-05-22
**Purpose:** Personal notes on the hold-first sequence for correcting retroactive leave

---

## The Problem

When a manager submits a retroactive change to an employee's leave start date — especially
after a payroll period has partially closed — the system sometimes triggers an automatic
overpayment-recovery letter before the correction is even confirmed. This causes unnecessary
distress to the employee and extra work for HR.

I went through this twice in the past quarter. The cases below are real, with employee names
included here for my own reference. These notes are NOT for publication as-is.

## The Correct Sequence (The Lesson)

Every one of these cases could have been handled without triggering the letter if the
sequence below had been followed. I have tested it and it works reliably.

**Step 1: Open the payroll hold first.**
Before making any date change, call the payroll team (ext. 2210) to place a manual hold
on the salary component for the affected employee. This prevents the automatic
overpayment-recovery calculation from running during the correction window.

**Step 2: Change the leave start date.**
With the hold in place, update the leave start date in the HR system. The system will
calculate the correction silently, without triggering the recovery letter.

**Step 3: Review the recalculation.**
The overnight batch recalculates the amounts. Check the result the next morning. If the
amount looks correct, proceed to step 4.

**Step 4: Agree on a repayment schedule (if any).**
If a repayment is owed, agree on the schedule with the employee. Splits of up to three
periods require no manager approval. Four or more periods need written approval from the
line manager. Document the agreement in the case notes.

**Step 5: Release the hold and call the employee.**
Release the payroll hold after the schedule is agreed. Call the employee before any letter
is generated. The system sends the recovery letter automatically once the hold is released,
so the employee must be prepared.

## Payroll Cutoff Date

**Important correction to the intranet page:**

The intranet page on payroll corrections (https://intranet.example.com/hr/payroll-corrections)
still states that the payroll cutoff is the **18th of the month**. This is wrong.

Since the January 2025 payroll system migration, the cutoff is the **22nd of the month**.
Corrections submitted after the 22nd will fall into the following month's run.

I have raised this with the HR Content team but the page has not been updated as of
2025-05-22. Until the page is corrected, please use the 22nd date in all communications.

---

## Repayment Schedule Rules (Summary)

- Up to 3 periods: no manager approval needed, HR Specialist can confirm.
- 4 or more periods: written approval from line manager required.
- Employee must always be called before the recovery letter is sent.
- Document the agreement in the HR case notes with the date and the employee's verbal consent.
